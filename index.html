Restaurant Rivals

prototype v1
    Quick rules
    • 5 days. Score = cash + $10 per star. Can't pay rent? You're out.
    • 6 regulars show up every day. Each star above 3 brings 2 maybe-customers; each star below 3 risks losing 2 regulars. Dice decide.
    • Ad cards go face down in front of any shop: real $6 (brings 2 customers), bluff $2 (brings nobody). Your rival only sees how many. You pay when they flip.
    • Before the flip, both sides can scramble: pop-up table $4 (today only), rush ingredients $3 each.
    • You can serve as many tables as your tables or ingredients allow, whichever is lower.
    • Each table pays $8 in the countryside, $12 in the city. Rent is your only fixed cost. Leftover food spoils.
    • Market: 24 ingredients a day. First 8 cost $1, next 8 $2, last 8 $3. Poorer player buys first.
    • Turn people away and you lose stars (1–3 people −1, 4+ −2). A $3 voucher saves one. Serve everyone: +1 star.
